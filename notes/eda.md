
# EDA

## Dataset Overview
The Komodo dataset covers 116.0 million patients and 5.3 million providers; about half of all patients (59.4 million) are aged 50+.

## HIV Population Aged 50+
Using the HIV definition, we found 286,720 people with HIV aged 50+, or 0.48% of all patients aged 50+.

## Hypertension by HIV Status
Hypertension was more common among people with HIV aged 50+ (77.9%) than among people without HIV (69.7%).
