Methods and how to read this site

Three runs

The full cohort is the result. The 1% and 30% samples were used to test the pipeline. Each sample is a fixed set of people chosen by a hash of the patient ID, so the 1% sample is inside the 30% one and the same people are picked every time.

Small numbers are hidden

Only aggregated counts leave the database, and no exact count is published. Any count from 1 to 10 is shown as <11 and the percentages on that row are hidden. Every other count is rounded to the nearest 10 (the smallest shown is 20), and percentages to one decimal, so a hidden count cannot be worked out by subtracting other numbers from a total. Zeros are shown as zeros.

Provisional

The HIV medicine rules, the condition code lists and the care quality codes are still being reviewed with the principal investigator, so treat every number here as provisional.

Matching

The propensity score is a logistic regression of HIV status on birth year, census region and race, fitted once for each year on every person with HIV and 20 randomly chosen controls of the same year and sex. Matching is exact on sex and one to one, nearest neighbour on the logit of the score, highest scores first, without replacement within a year. Balance is the standardized mean difference, scaled by the standard deviation in the HIV group.

Gagne score and care quality

The Gagne score uses Nemo Zhou's ICD-10-CM lookups; a diagnosis counts in the calendar year it falls in. Care quality measures look back further where the measure needs it, for example an eye exam in the last two years.
